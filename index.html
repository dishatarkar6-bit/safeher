The Problem We Address

In an unsafe situation, people may panic and waste valuable time searching for the right emergency service or sharing their location. SafeHer aims to make essential safety actions easier to access.

How SafeHer Works

Three simple steps to access important safety support.

1

Open SafeHer

Access the platform from a phone or computer whenever safety support is needed.

2

Choose an Action

Use SOS, emergency call, location sharing or nearby help according to the situation.

3

Reach Support

Connect with emergency services, trusted people or nearby assistance.

🛡️ Safety Tips

📱 Keep your phone charged and accessible.
👥 Share your travel plans with someone you trust.
📍 Keep location services available when travelling.
🚶 Avoid isolated areas whenever possible.
❤️ Trust your instincts if a situation feels unsafe.
